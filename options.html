Tab protection

These preferences control which tabs can be considered for unloading.

Startup and time limits

Discovery, loading waits, and tab attempts all share the overall deadline. If it expires, no new unload attempt starts.

Validation rule: startup discovery plus loading wait must fit inside the overall deadline, and the per-tab time budget cannot exceed the overall deadline.
